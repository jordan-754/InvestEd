Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 30, 2025

At InvestEd, we take your financial education and data privacy seriously. This policy explains how we handle student information.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide the InvestEd experience:

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • Student ID or School name (to verify eligibility)
  • App usage data (to track your learning progress)

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to personalize your investment goals, track your saving streaks, and provide support. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Security

All transaction data and personal info are protected using 256-bit encryption. We follow industry standards to ensure your "micro-investments" are safe.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about your data, please contact the InvestEd team at support@invested-ph.com.
